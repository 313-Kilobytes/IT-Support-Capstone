# Week 4: AI Career Readiness and Final Presentation

## Overview

Week 4 focuses on finalising the IT Support Capstone Project by preparing the project for professional presentation and submission. This phase combines the technical work completed in Weeks 1 to 3 with career readiness materials and a funding-style presentation for Ubuntu Innovations (Pty) Ltd.

The purpose of this week is to demonstrate both technical competence and professional communication. The deliverables are designed to show how the proposed IT infrastructure solution supports Ubuntu Innovations as a growing South African technology company seeking funding for secure and scalable business operations.

## Objectives

The objectives for Week 4 are to:

* Package the complete IT infrastructure proposal professionally.
* Present Ubuntu Innovations as a real company seeking funding.
* Communicate the business value of the proposed IT solution.
* Prepare individual career readiness documents.
* Demonstrate readiness for entry-level IT Support opportunities.
* Consolidate all capstone deliverables into a final submission format.

## Deliverables

This folder contains the Week 4 deliverables required for the final phase of the capstone project.

### 1. Resume / IT Support CV

The IT Support CV presents the candidate’s technical skills, project experience, training background, and career readiness for entry-level IT Support roles.

It highlights relevant skills such as:

* Technical troubleshooting
* Networking fundamentals
* Operating system administration
* Cybersecurity awareness
* Customer support
* Technical documentation
* GitHub and portfolio-based project work

### 2. Cover Letter

The cover letter is tailored for an entry-level IT Support role. It explains the candidate’s interest in IT support, relevant technical training, software development background, and ability to assist users in a professional environment.

### 3. LinkedIn Summary

The LinkedIn summary provides a professional profile description that can be used on LinkedIn or adapted for other career platforms. It focuses on IT support, technical troubleshooting, continuous learning, and the candidate’s transition into technology roles.

### 4. Interview Questions and Answers

This document contains ten common IT Support interview questions with structured sample answers. The questions cover areas such as:

* Troubleshooting
* Networking
* Customer service
* Cybersecurity
* Backups
* User permissions
* Technical communication
* Career motivation

The purpose of this section is to support interview preparation and demonstrate readiness for workplace scenarios.

### 5. Final Presentation Slides

The presentation has been adapted into a funding-style pitch for Ubuntu Innovations (Pty) Ltd.

Instead of presenting the work only as a technical project, the slides position Ubuntu Innovations as a real growth-stage company seeking funding to implement secure and scalable IT infrastructure.

The presentation covers:

1. Ubuntu Innovations funding proposal
2. Business overview
3. The business problem
4. Why IT infrastructure matters
5. Funding use: hardware and software
6. Secure network design
7. Data protection and access control
8. Backup, recovery and business continuity
9. Cybersecurity and risk reduction
10. Funding request, impact and closing

### 6. Mock LinkedIn Company Profile

The mock LinkedIn-style company profile presents Ubuntu Innovations as a professional organisation. It supports the funding pitch by giving the company a more realistic business identity and showing how it could present itself publicly.

## Presentation Purpose

The final presentation is structured as if Ubuntu Innovations is pitching to potential funders or stakeholders.

The goal is to show that the proposed IT infrastructure is not just a technical expense, but a strategic investment that supports:

* Business growth
* Data protection
* Employee productivity
* Cybersecurity
* Operational reliability
* Business continuity
* Investor confidence

Ubuntu Innovations requires secure, scalable, and professionally managed IT infrastructure to support its next phase of growth. The proposed solution reduces operational risk, protects sensitive data, supports employees, and provides a reliable foundation for future expansion.
